# Changes 2026-11-21 — what and why (for Gemini's audit)

Commits: `2b10b26` (deep-dive fixes 1–5 + docs), `049ac28` (minScore default).
All changes were requested from your MODEL-DEEP-DIVE.md audit, then verified in the
sandbox (typecheck clean; `/api/meta`, `/api/debug/cell`, tiles, `/api/hotspots`,
`/api/shop`, `/api/reward/claim` smoke-tested; A/B against `743a880` showed no
regression — the new code gives equal or higher habitat scores on identical input).

## 1. `src/habitat.ts` — specialist hard vetoes

**What:** in `staticHabitatScore`, before S_env:
`if (plasticity <= 0.30 && (fSoil < 0.20 || fDepth < 0.20)) return 0`
(flagged as `specialistVeto` in `/api/debug/cell`).

**Why:** low-plasticity species (sommartryffel 0.15, jättemusseron 0.25, korallticka 0.30)
need near-exact soil type and soil depth. The soft floor alone still let them score on
wrong soil; a hard veto stops false hotspots for rare/niche species. Generalists
(plasticity ≥ 0.5) are never vetoed, so kantarell/trattkantarell keep their wide range.

## 2. `src/habitat.ts` — inverted soft floor

**What:** `floor = 0.05 + 0.25·(1−p)` → `floor = 0.02 + 0.28·p`.

**Why:** the old formula was backwards — specialists (low p) got the *higher* floor
(0.26 at p=0.15) and generalists the *lower* one (0.09 at p=0.85), i.e. the model was
most forgiving exactly where it should be strict. Now: generalist p=0.9 → floor 0.27
(forgiving), specialist p=0.15 → floor 0.06 (near-binary). `soft(x)=max(floor,x)`
semantics unchanged.

## 3. `src/habitat.ts` — S_env weights rebalanced (no hydrological double-count)

**What:** moisture .17→.15, age .13→.15, canopy .08→.09, soil .09→.10, TWI .06→.04,
waterHollow .05→.03, waterDist .04→.03 (standStruct .05, deadwood .05, SIS/climate/
depth/terrain .07 each). Sum still 1.00.

**Why:** markfuktighet (class raster) + TWI (continuous, from 2 m DEM) + water_rel +
water_dist all measure wetness — together they were ~.32 of S_env, drowning out
age/soil. Hydrology now totals .25. Age was raised because age is the better maturity
proxy than tree height (age ≥ 80 already compensates low height on hällmark/tallhed).
TWI keeps its own .04 because it captures real micro-topography the class raster misses.

## 4. `src/server.ts` — saturating rain curves

**What** (in `computeCells`, `/api/debug/cell`, and the `/api/meta` display):
- rain14: `min(1, rain14/60)` → `1 − exp(−rain14/32)` (half-saturation ≈ 22 mm/14 d)
- rain90: `0.75 + 0.25·min(1, rain90/200)` → `0.75 + 0.25·(1 − exp(−rain90/120))`

**Why:** the linear ramps gave full marks at 60 mm/14 d — a single skyfall maxed the
fruiting trigger, which is ecologically wrong (light rain already triggers fruiting;
extra rain has diminishing returns and can waterlog). Exponential saturation caps that.
The 90-day term keeps its 0.75 baseline so a dry late summer still suppresses the score,
but saturates instead of ramping linearly. Verified live: rain14=31.2, rain90=187.3 →
rainFactor 0.591, matching the formula exactly.

## 5. `src/server.ts` — obsPrior capped + protected species zeroed

**What:** σ 250 m → 400 m, cutoff 750 m → 1200 m, boost `1 + 0.35·prior` →
`1 + 0.15·prior`, and `prior = 0` forced for `species.protected` (jättemusseron,
fridlyst/VU) in both tile scoring and `/api/debug/cell`.

**Why:** +35 % at σ 250 m turned the map into a point map around known Artportalen
occurrences and — worse — risked overcollection pressure on rare/protected species
whose (blurred) occurrences lit up the map. The wider, weaker prior keeps a soft
"this area is known good" signal without dominating the habitat score. Protected
species never get a prior boost: their locations must not glow on the map.

## 6. `src/server.ts` — hotspots/peaks default minScore 80 → 50 (`049ac28`)

**What:** `Number(req.query.minScore ?? 80) || 80` → `?? 50` in both `/api/hotspots`
and `/api/peaks`.

**Why:** this was the "no mushrooms show in the iOS app" bug. Dynamic score =
habitat × season × rain × frost × flush × (1+0.15·prior) rarely exceeds ~60–70, so a
default threshold of 80 always returned `count: 0` pins — while `static=1` tiles
(score = habitat×100, up to 99) showed heat fine. The map looked alive, the pins were
empty. 50 catches genuinely good cells in dynamic mode; with `static=1` the client can
pass a higher threshold (e.g. 80) for only the hottest habitat. Verified: `static=1`
→ 120 pins, max score 99–100; dynamic demo max ~35 (low bbox-center rain), real data
higher.

Related operational finding: `computeCells` samples SMHI rain once at the **bbox
center** and applies it to all cells — rain14=15 mm at the center vs 31 mm at a point
scaled scores 57 → 34 in the sandbox test. Acceptable approximation for a ≤4° viewport,
but worth knowing when reading `/api/debug/cell` (which samples at the exact point).
